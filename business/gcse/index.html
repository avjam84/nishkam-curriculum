Ethos

Virtue-led Business learning

Business Studies at Nishkam is not only about passing exams. Every topic connects to who we are — a community learning to serve, to think carefully and to act with integrity.

Faith-Inspired

“Learning from the wisdom of all traditions”

Businesses that act with integrity and compassion reflect values at the heart of every great faith. In GCSE Business we ask what a business is for — profit alone, or also service to customers, workers and the community.

Virtues-Led

“Fostering compassion, humility, justice and courage”

Every business decision is an ethical choice — a chance to practise virtue or fall short of it. Ethics versus profit, fair treatment of employees and honest marketing all appear in the specification.

Aspiring for Excellence

“Becoming the best human beings we can possibly be”

In Business Studies, this means thinking critically, writing with precision, and achieving Grade 9.

“To empower children with virtues that enable them to excel academically and spiritually, inspiring them to serve humanity selflessly — with an abundance of love, compassion and forgiveness.”

— Nishkam School Trust · Mission Statement

Virtues

Peacefulness · Diligence · Determination

Our three classroom virtues, and what they look like in Business.

In Business

Peacefulness

Approach complex questions with a calm, focused mind. Listen to different stakeholder perspectives with respect. Create a learning environment where every voice is valued.

In Business

Diligence

Commit fully to mastering each topic. Check your chains of analysis, refine your evaluations, and never submit work that is less than your best.

In Business

Determination

Persevere through challenging 9 and 12-mark questions. Treat every mistake as a stepping stone. Keep pushing toward your target grade and beyond.
