Ethos

Our Curriculum Pillars

Faith-Inspired

Learning from the wisdom of all traditions. Literature carries the voices of many cultures and eras — from Shakespeare and the Romantics to Hosseini and Atwood — and we read them with openness and respect.

Virtues-Led

Fostering compassion, humility, justice and courage. Through characters who struggle with compassion, responsibility and power, students learn to speak about virtue with precision.

Aspiring for Excellence

Becoming the best human beings we can possibly be. Redrafting, feedback and close reading build articulate, confident readers and writers.

Nishkam School Trust mission

“To empower children with virtues that enable them to excel academically and spiritually, inspiring them to serve humanity selflessly — with an abundance of love, compassion and forgiveness.”
— Nishkam School Trust · Mission Statement
Virtues

Three Virtues in English

Peacefulness

Close reading needs a calm, patient mind: slowing down to notice a single word, and listening respectfully to interpretations different from our own.

Diligence

Excellent writing is redrafted. Learning quotations, practising timed essays and acting on feedback are steady daily work.

Determination

Unseen texts and demanding set works can feel difficult; determination means returning to them until meaning opens up.
